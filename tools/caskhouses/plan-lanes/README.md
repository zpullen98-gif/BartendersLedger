# The lane files

21 November 2026. These six files are the planners' record for wave one of the producers by style, whisky and brandy: american-bourbon, american-other, scotch-regions, scotch-categories, irish-japanese-world and brandy. Each holds its groups of five picks, two alternates a group, and what its planner left and why.

They are kept as written and nothing reads them. The roster is `tools/caskhouses/plan.json`, merged from them the same day: it holds the groups whose five picks stand, their chunks, any swap, and every left entry. A change to a pick is made in plan.json and recorded in the group's swaps; an alternate is looked up here.
