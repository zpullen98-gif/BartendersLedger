Every new reader starts with Learn why. The multiple-choice and general recall
readers expand beside the answer. Their full reading links use existing routes;
Back restores the same answered screen and position. Fall, Compass and Brew
recall already linked to complete entries; those links now share the label.
The reader never records an attempt. Spoken exercises still require Record it.

Accuracy and accessibility changes
- House quiz adapters preserve exact item IDs and card keys. An overlapping
  drink name cannot silently send this new reader to a different house drink.
- Pairing context includes the actual dish description and ingredients, the
  wine's kept profile, serving advice and food connections, and the appropriate
  wine or zero-proof reason. Formula labels follow dish, wine or cocktail kind.
- Kept house offers are recommendations to discuss with the guest. The correct
  option does not imply that every alternative pairing is intrinsically wrong.
- Say it back and guest exercises explain the limits of keyword checking; an
  accurate paraphrase should be compared with the underlying service principle.
- Low-score feedback invites explanation and practice. Service feedback no
  longer says all three when fewer than three service fields were recorded.
- New body text is at least 16px; disclosure and link targets are at least 44px.
  Native disclosures support keyboard use. Quiz Enter/Space/arrow shortcuts
  do not advance or grade while focus is inside the explanation.
- Imported text is escaped, and AI drafts are excluded from kept explanations.

Source limits
No house recipe, measure, glassware, garnish, allergen conclusion, historical
claim or producer fact was invented or changed in this pass. The current pack
does not supply glassware for the 32 house drinks, so no glassware questions
are added. Five drinks have a recorded method or garnish and can still use
Service details. Empty service fields remain unavailable; library standards
are explicitly distinguished from the current house's specifications.
The current records are the source of these descriptions, not a new independent
verification of every statement in the house pack. Updates remain the team's
responsibility. Learning and allergy decisions should use current house notes.

Automated content audit
The browser audit deals every eligible item once per generated family, with a
fixed random stream for shared-engine families. It is not every possible
combination of stems and distractors.
- 160 house drink templates: 32 each name, specification, tableside line,
  offer-next and parts.
- 86 pairing questions: 43 wine and 43 zero-proof.
- 95 producer questions: 23 producer-of, 36 place, 36 used-in.
- 207 coffee/tea questions from all 69 entries.
- 548 generated/entry questions total: no repeated choices within a question,
  missing answer readers or lost pairing IDs.
- Recall inventory: 362 word cards, 824 component cards, 108 producer cards,
  119 Fall cards, 128 Compass cards and 69 Brew cards.
- Spoken inventory: 32 house drinks and 23 guest/mix-up exercises.
